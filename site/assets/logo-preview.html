Mark + wordmark.

This pass.

  • Form. A single bold gold G with a centred downward triangle — the plumb-bob, abstracted to its minimum.
  • Why a plumb-bob. The classical construction tool for finding vertical truth. The mark says: structured form (G) anchored to a true reference (the bob).
  • Sans-serif mark, serif site. Mark and wordmark are sans (Inter / Helvetica stack, with a gold full-stop on the wordmark). Site body and headlines stay Georgia.
  • Three variants shipped. Gold on dark (primary), deeper gold on bone (light bg / print), single-fill currentColor (for inline SVG that inherits text color).
