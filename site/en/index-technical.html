01 / 06   THE PROBLEM

Construction runs on five vendor platforms.

The standards are open. The platforms on which they live are not.

buildingSMART has standardised the data formats of the building industry since 1995 — IFC for models, BCF for coordination, IDS for requirements. The formats are open and ISO-certified.

What no standard ever defined is the platform on which these formats are exchanged. Five companies filled that gap. Today they own how your coordination, audit trail and project memory are stored.

02 / 06   THE LAYER

An open layer. Not a platform.

Standards stay where they are. Storage stays where you want it. Authority moves to keys.

PRINCIPLE 001

Signed.

Every action — topic, comment, status change, diary entry — is cryptographically signed by its author. Identity is a keypair you control, not an account a vendor can suspend.

PRINCIPLE 002

Federated.

Models, documents and events live where their owner decides — on-prem, self-hosted, or with a provider of choice. No single platform holds the authoritative copy. Migration is a config change, not a project.

PRINCIPLE 003

Standards‑true.

IFC stays IFC. BCF stays BCF. IDS stays IDS. Gemeinwert is the wire format between buildingSMART standards and signed records — it adds nothing the standards forbid.
