gemeinwert / bimbeam

Bimbeam is the first provider on the open layer.

Bimbeam turns the BIM-CVP protocol into practical HKLS / MEP, BIM coordination and public-procurement support work for real projects.

What Bimbeam does.

  • HKLS / MEP planning and BIM coordination.
  • IFC export setup, property sets and IDS validation loops.
  • BCF issue workflows and openCDE handover preparation.
  • CAM Edilizia evidence strategy for Italian public projects.
  • Building logbook and asset handover structure.

Why this belongs outside the protocol.

The provider should not own the protocol. Bimbeam can sell competence and project work, while BIM-CVP remains MIT licensed and usable by other offices, public clients and software vendors.

That separation is what makes adoption easier: the market can copy the protocol without copying the company.
